- Purr is quicker:
  - Saving to its database no longer waits for the disk after every write, about 0.2 ms each instead of 7.
  - A big diff lays out about 6 times faster when you open or close a file, a comment or hidden lines.
  - A poll no longer rewrites PRs you gave a verdict on.
  - The PR's header skips reads it doesn't show.
  - Marking a PR redraws only its row.
  - The `/` suggestions no longer reload the PR shown.
